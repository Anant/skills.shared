# Search Query Builder

Generate queries by combining: **topic × beneficiary × geography × applicant type × funding type × deadline terms × funder language**.

## Core Query Patterns
- "[topic]" "grant" "[geography]"
- "[topic]" "fellowship" "application"
- "[topic]" "request for proposals"
- "[topic]" "funding opportunity"
- "[topic]" "foundation" "grant"
- "[beneficiary]" "workforce development grant"
- "[topic]" "curriculum development" "grant"
- "[topic]" "public interest technology" "fellowship"
- "[topic]" "AI governance" "funding"
- "[topic]" "AI safety" "grant"
- "[topic]" "open source" "funding"
- "[topic]" "digital equity" "grant"
- "[topic]" "responsible AI" "fellowship"

## Modifier Vocabularies (swap into patterns)
- **Deadline terms:** "deadline" · "apply by" · "rolling" · "letter of inquiry" · "LOI" · "now accepting" · "call for proposals" · "NOFO"
- **Funder language:** "grantmaking" · "program areas" · "we fund" · "areas of focus" · "apply for funding"
- **Policy language:** "responsible AI" · "trustworthy AI" · "AI accountability" · "algorithmic governance" · "digital sovereignty"
- **Education language:** "curriculum" · "professional development" · "upskilling" · "adult learning" · "open educational resources" · "train-the-trainer"
- **Research language:** "research grant" · "seed funding" · "pilot study" · "evaluation" · "field building"
- **Applicant-type terms:** "nonprofit" · "individuals" · "small business" · "fiscally sponsored" · "independent researchers"

## Recency and Quality Filters (apply mentally or via engine tools)
- Prefer results from funder domains over aggregator blogs.
- Check page dates; discard opportunities announced >18 months ago unless recurring.
- For recurring programs, search "[program name] [current year]".
