# Education, Curriculum, and Workforce Development Sources

> **Verification reminder:** Education funders often restrict by geography, grade level, or institution type. Verify eligibility line-by-line, especially "K-12 only" vs. adult/professional learning.

## Government (detail in government-sources.md)
- Department of Education (adult ed, innovation, postsecondary)
- Department of Labor (workforce, apprenticeship, upskilling)
- NSF education directorates (STEM education research and practice)
- IMLS (library-partnered community learning)
- State workforce boards and digital equity offices

## Foundation Archetypes
### Education innovation funders
- **Useful for:** Curriculum development, learning R&D, edtech pilots
- **Typical opportunities:** $25k–$500k program grants; some open challenges
- **Complexity notes:** Level 3–4
- **Where to look:** national education foundations, learning-science funders, edtech philanthropy. [verify per funder]

### Workforce and future-of-work funders
- **Useful for:** Adult upskilling, career pathways, employer-linked training — strong AI-literacy fit
- **Where to look:** foundations with future-of-work portfolios; corporate foundations of large employers; regional workforce funder collaboratives. [verify per funder]

### Adult learning and digital literacy funders
- **Useful for:** Community-based AI literacy, library programs, digital inclusion
- **Complexity notes:** Often Level 2–3, community-scale grants

### Teacher/facilitator training funders
- **Useful for:** Train-the-trainer models, educator professional development
- **Where to look:** education foundations, humanities councils, subject-matter associations

### Open educational resources (OER) funders
- **Useful for:** Openly licensed curriculum — strong fit for open-source-aligned initiatives
- **Where to look:** open education programs at foundations, university OER initiatives, government open-textbook programs. [verify per program]
