## Project
- Project name: Intelcraft AI Education & Enablement Program

- Full description: Intelcraft develops and delivers applied AI education — curriculum, cohort workshops, study halls, office hours, and open knowledge bases — and supports research and public-interest work on AI sovereignty, AI alignment, AI governance, local AI capacity, knowledge management, open-source AI tools, and human-centered AI adoption.

- Use of funds: Curriculum development, facilitation, open educational resources, community programs, evaluation

## Applicant Eligibility Preferences
- Nonprofit-only acceptable? Yes (via fiscal sponsor)
- Individual fellowships acceptable? Yes (for founder/curriculum lead)
- For-profit grants acceptable? Yes, if mission-aligned
- Fiscal sponsor required? Available if needed
- University partner required? Acceptable, not preferred
- Government grants acceptable? Yes, if effort ≤ Level 4
- International grants acceptable? Yes, remote-eligible only
- State/local grants acceptable? Yes
- Corporate sponsorship acceptable? Yes (with pedagogical-neutrality disclosure)
- Prizes/challenges acceptable? Yes

## Application Preferences
- Maximum time to apply: Level 3 (1 week) preferred; Level 4 for ≥$75k
- Deadline window: Next 90 days + rolling
- Rolling preferred: Yes
- LOI preferred: Yes
- Short form preferred: Yes
- Full proposal acceptable: For ≥$50k
- Budget required: Draft available
- Letters of support available: 2–3 obtainable within a week
- Audited financials available: No
- Evaluation plan available: Draft
- Prior results available: Pilot testimonials and participation counts

## Structural Requirements
- Reporting burden tolerance: Medium
- Match requirement acceptable? Up to 25% in-kind
- Indirect cost minimum needed: 10%
- Allowed expenses needed: Staff, contractors, stipends, tools
- Disallowed expenses to avoid: Capital, lobbying

## Strategic
- Probability threshold to pursue: ≥ moderate, unless strategic value ≥8
- Strategic relationship value sought: AI governance funder network; education funder credibility
- Proposal readiness level: 6/10 (assembly required)

## Exclusions
- Excluded funders: Any requiring exclusive platform endorsement conflicting with pedagogical neutrality
- Excluded geographies: Programs requiring in-person delivery outside reachable regions
- Excluded topics: Pure capabilities research without public-interest framing
- Excluded application types: Level 5 federal grants this cycle (no audited financials)
- Ethical restrictions: No funders whose requirements would compromise open licensing of core curriculum
