|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Confidence values (use exactly one per row):**
- **Verified from primary source** — confirmed on the funder's own page/document.
- **Verified from secondary source** — confirmed on a reputable aggregator/news source; primary check still pending.
- **Unverified** — found but not yet checked.
- **Stale / needs checking** — source is dated or cycle may have passed.
- **Not enough information** — key fields cannot be confirmed from available sources.

**Rules**
- No opportunity appears in a shortlist or report as "open" without a primary-source row here.
- Re-verify anything older than 30 days before it enters a handoff brief.
- Never fabricate or interpolate deadlines; if unknown, write `UNKNOWN — verify`.
