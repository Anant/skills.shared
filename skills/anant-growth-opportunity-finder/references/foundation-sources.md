# Foundation and Philanthropy Sources

> **Verification reminder:** Many foundations are invitation-only or accept LOIs only in windows. Check each foundation's "How to apply" page; a program area listed on a website does not mean open applications.

## Research Tools
- **Candid / Foundation Directory** — funder search by subject/geo/size; grant history reveals real (vs. stated) priorities. [verify — candid.org]
- **990 lookups (Candid, ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer)** — free way to see who a foundation actually funded and at what size. [verify — projects.propublica.org/nonprofits]
- **Philanthropy News Digest RFPs** — free live RFP feed. [verify]

## Foundation Types and How to Work Them

### Private / national foundations
- **Useful for:** Larger programmatic grants; multi-year support
- **Typical opportunities:** $50k–$1M+; LOI → invited proposal
- **Best-fit themes for this skill's users:** technology & society, responsible AI, future of work, education innovation, democracy
- **Complexity notes:** Level 3–5; relationships matter; many invitation-only
- **Approach:** identify program officers via staff pages; mirror program language; start with LOI or intro

### Family foundations
- **Useful for:** Flexible, smaller grants; often local or founder-interest driven
- **Complexity notes:** Level 1–3; frequently no website — find via 990s
- **Approach:** letter + relationship; emphasize concrete community outcomes

### Community foundations
- **Useful for:** Place-based work; fiscal sponsorship pathways; donor-advised fund (DAF) introductions
- **Typical opportunities:** $2k–$50k competitive cycles; DAF gifts of any size
- **Complexity notes:** Level 2–3; geography is the primary filter
- **Approach:** align to local priorities (workforce, digital access, small business)

### Donor-advised funds (DAFs)
- **Useful for:** Individual-donor pathways at scale; no application in the usual sense
- **Approach:** cultivate individual donors and provide a giving page/sponsor; note DAFs cannot fund individuals directly

## Prospecting Workflow
1. Search Candid/990s for funders of 3–5 peer organizations.
2. Build a 20-funder prospect list with program areas, typical grant size, and application pathway.
3. Sort into: Open application / LOI window / Invitation-only (relationship-build).
4. Verify each open pathway on the funder's site; log in the source verification log.
