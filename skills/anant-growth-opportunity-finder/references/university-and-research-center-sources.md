# University and Research Center Sources

> **Verification reminder:** Center funding is often internal-only or requires a faculty PI. Verify whether external individuals/organizations can apply directly, need a faculty collaborator, or can only receive subawards.

## What Universities Offer External Partners
1. **Center grants and RFPs** — some AI/ethics/policy centers run open calls for affiliated research or education projects. [verify per center]
2. **Visiting fellowships / practitioner residencies** — stipended affiliations for builders and researchers. [verify per center]
3. **Subawards on larger grants** — universities holding federal awards can subcontract curriculum, training, or community components — pitch as a delivery partner.
4. **Co-proposal partnerships** — many federal programs require or favor academic partners; a university PI can unlock otherwise-ineligible opportunities.
5. **Fellowship office listings** — curated external-fellowship databases (see fellowship-sources.md).

## Center Archetypes to Search
- University AI institutes (research + outreach funds)
- AI ethics/governance centers (policy fellowships, workshops)
- Law school technology programs (policy and governance work)
- Education schools / learning-science labs (curriculum research)
- Public policy schools (civic tech, governance fellowships)
- Computer science departments with broadening-participation programs
- University innovation/entrepreneurship centers (founder support)

## Think Tanks and Research Institutes
- Technology policy programs at major think tanks — fellowships, project funding, and convening budgets; also strong proposal partners. [verify per institute]
- Independent AI research institutes — occasional open calls, workshops with honoraria, collaboration pathways. [verify]

## How to Work This Channel
1. Identify 5–10 centers aligned to the thesis.
2. For each: check "funding," "fellowships," "call for proposals," and "affiliates" pages.
3. Where no open call exists, log as **relationship-build** with the specific partnership angle (subaward, co-proposal, guest curriculum).
4. Record faculty whose work matches — named collaborators strengthen federal proposals.
