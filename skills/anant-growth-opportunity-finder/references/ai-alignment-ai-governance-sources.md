# AI Alignment, AI Safety, AI Governance, and AI Sovereignty Sources

> **Verification reminder:** This field's funding landscape changes fast — new programs launch and close within months. Verify every program's current status directly; check announcement dates and prefer pages updated within the last 12 months.

## Funder Archetypes (search each)

### Dedicated AI safety grantmakers
- **Useful for:** Technical alignment research, evaluations, field-building, safety education
- **Typical opportunities:** Research grants ($10k–$1M+), individual support, career-transition funding
- **Complexity notes:** Often Level 2–3 (short forms, rolling); some fund individuals directly — unusual and valuable
- **Where to look:** philanthropies with dedicated AI safety/global catastrophic risk programs; regranting funds run by AI safety organizations; rapid-turnaround "micro-grant" programs in the safety community. [verify per funder]

### AI governance and policy funders
- **Useful for:** Governance research, policy capacity, standards work, governance training
- **Typical opportunities:** Institutional grants, policy fellowships, convening support
- **Where to look:** foundations with technology-and-democracy or responsible-tech programs; national security/emerging tech philanthropy; think-tank funders. [verify per funder]

### Responsible AI / tech-and-society programs at major foundations
- **Useful for:** Public-interest AI, AI and civil rights, AI in public systems
- **Complexity notes:** Level 3–5; usually organizational grants, often invitation-preferred
- **Where to look:** large national foundations' technology, democracy, or future-of-work portfolios. [verify per funder]

### AI policy institutes and university centers
- **Useful for:** Affiliations, subawards, fellowships, co-authored proposals
- **Where to look:** university AI governance/safety centers, ethics institutes, law-school tech programs (see university-and-research-center-sources.md). [verify per center]

### Government AI programs
- **Useful for:** Trustworthy AI, standards, evaluation, workforce (NIST, NSF, digital equity flows)
- **Complexity notes:** Level 4–5 (see government-sources.md)

### AI security and sovereignty programs
- **Useful for:** Local AI capacity, digital sovereignty, public AI infrastructure
- **Where to look:** digital public infrastructure funders, sovereign tech funds (see open-source and international files), national digital agencies. [verify per program]

## Fit Screening
Apply `rubrics/ai-sovereignty-alignment-rubric.md` to every funder found here. Watch for two common mismatches:
1. **Capabilities vs. safety:** some "AI grants" fund AI adoption/capabilities, not safety/governance — read the program language.
2. **Research vs. education:** many safety funders prioritize research over curriculum; if the project is educational, lead with field-building and talent-pipeline framing.
