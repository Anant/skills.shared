# Corporate and Ecosystem Funding Sources

> **Verification reminder:** Corporate programs rebrand and re-scope constantly. Verify the current program page, and check whether the award is cash, credits, or in-kind — the difference matters for budgets.

## Categories

### Corporate foundations
- **Useful for:** Cash grants aligned to CSR priorities (workforce, digital skills, community)
- **Typical opportunities:** $5k–$250k; annual cycles or invitation
- **Complexity notes:** Level 2–4; employee/market-presence geography often matters
- **Where to look:** foundations of major tech, telecom, financial, and retail employers — search "[company] foundation grants." [verify per company]

### Cloud and AI platform credits
- **Useful for:** Non-dilutive infrastructure support (compute for AI education/research)
- **Typical opportunities:** Credits programs for startups, nonprofits, researchers, and educators
- **Complexity notes:** Level 1–2; fast; not cash — pair with cash funders
- **Where to look:** every major cloud/AI provider runs startup, research, and nonprofit credit programs. [verify per provider]

### Developer and ecosystem grants
- **Useful for:** Building on a platform, open tooling, developer education
- **Complexity notes:** Level 2; deliverable-oriented
- **Where to look:** platform developer-relations grant programs, ecosystem funds. [verify]

### Startup / accelerator programs
- **Useful for:** Founder-stage support, sometimes non-dilutive
- **Complexity notes:** Level 2–3; check equity terms carefully

### Social-impact sponsorships
- **Useful for:** Event, cohort, or program sponsorship from companies' marketing/CSR budgets
- **Complexity notes:** Level 1–2; relationship-driven; write a one-page sponsorship menu
- **Approach:** pitch audience access + brand alignment, not charity

### Tech-for-nonprofits programs
- **Useful for:** Discounted/free software and services (stretches budgets in proposals)
- **Where to look:** nonprofit tech marketplaces and vendor nonprofit programs. [verify]

## Positioning Notes
- Corporate funders respond to: measurable reach, employee-engagement hooks, brand-safe storytelling, and alignment with their market.
- For AI education projects, platform-agnostic curriculum can still accept platform credits — disclose relationships and preserve pedagogical neutrality in writing.
