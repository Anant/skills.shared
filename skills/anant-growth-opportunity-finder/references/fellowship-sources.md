# Fellowship Sources

> **Verification reminder:** Fellowships are cohort-based with hard annual deadlines that move year to year. Verify the current cycle's dates on the program page. Note whether the fellowship funds individuals directly (tax implications) or through institutions.

## Directories and Aggregators
- **ProFellow** — large searchable fellowship database; free account tier. Good first sweep. [verify — profellow.com]
- **Opportunity Desk** — global opportunities feed (fellowships, grants, prizes); strength in international/early-career. [verify — opportunitydesk.org]
- **University fellowship offices** — public listings of external fellowships maintained by graduate schools; excellent curated lists even for non-students. [verify per university]
- **Policy fellowship directories** — lists maintained by policy schools and civic organizations covering government-adjacent fellowships. [verify]
- **Founder fellowship directories** — startup/founder-focused lists (accelerator-adjacent). [verify]

## Categories Relevant to AI Education / Governance / Public Interest

### Public-interest technology fellowships
- **Useful for:** Technologists working on civic/public problems
- **Typical opportunities:** Stipended 6–24 month placements or project fellowships
- **Complexity notes:** Level 3–4; essays + references
- **Where to look:** public-interest tech networks and their member lists; civic tech organizations; foundations with tech-and-society programs. [verify per program]

### AI safety / AI governance fellowships
- **Useful for:** Research or career-transition support in alignment, safety, policy, evaluation
- **Typical opportunities:** Research fellowships, policy placements, career-change programs
- **Complexity notes:** Level 3–4; often rolling or 1–2 cycles/year; some fund individuals directly
- **Where to look:** AI safety funders and field-building organizations (see ai-alignment-ai-governance-sources.md); university AI centers; effective-altruism-adjacent funders. [verify per program]

### Research fellowships
- **Useful for:** Independent researchers and academics
- **Complexity notes:** Level 4–5; institutional affiliation sometimes required — check whether independent scholars are eligible

### Education fellowships
- **Useful for:** Educators, curriculum designers, education entrepreneurs
- **Complexity notes:** Level 2–4; often practitioner-friendly

### Civic innovation fellowships
- **Useful for:** Community leaders building local capacity
- **Complexity notes:** Level 2–3; local presence typically required

### Journalism / media fellowships
- **Useful for:** Public communication of AI topics; explainer and curriculum-adjacent work
- **Complexity notes:** Level 2–4

## Screening Questions for Any Fellowship
1. Does it fund individuals directly, or require an org/institution?
2. Is it a stipend, project budget, or both? Amount and duration?
3. Residency/relocation requirements?
4. Time commitment vs. the applicant's other work?
5. IP and publication expectations?
6. Alumni network value (strategic score input)?
