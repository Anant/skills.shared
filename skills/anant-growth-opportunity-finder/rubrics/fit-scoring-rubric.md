# Fit Scoring Rubric (100-Point Composite)

Score each criterion 0–10, multiply by its weight ÷ 10, sum to a 0–100 composite. Record evidence for every score.

| Criterion | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---:|---|
| Mission fit | 20 | Does the funder's stated purpose match the project's purpose (not just its keywords)? |
| Eligibility fit | 15 | Does the applicant clearly meet every eligibility rule? (Unclear = max 5.) |
| Topic fit | 15 | Does the funder fund this specific topic and activity type (e.g., curriculum creation, not just delivery)? |
| Beneficiary fit | 10 | Does the funder prioritize these beneficiaries/communities? |
| Funding size fit | 10 | Is the award within the useful range (≥ minimum useful size; effort proportional)? |
| Speed to apply | 10 | Inverse of application effort (level 1 → 10 pts; level 5 → 2 pts). |
| Probability of success | 10 | Competition level, applicant track record vs. typical grantees, relationship warmth. |
| Strategic value | 10 | Beyond the money: credibility, network, recurring potential, door-opening. |

## Scoring Anchors (per criterion, 0–10)
- **9–10:** Explicit, documented match; funder language mirrors the project.
- **7–8:** Strong match with minor tailoring needed.
- **5–6:** Plausible match requiring reframing or a partner.
- **3–4:** Stretch; only worth it if strategic value is high.
- **0–2:** Mismatch or unverifiable.

## Interpretation Bands
- **90–100:** Excellent fit. Apply immediately.
- **75–89:** Strong fit. Apply if capacity exists.
- **60–74:** Possible fit. Requires tailoring or partner.
- **40–59:** Weak fit. Monitor or relationship-build.
- **Below 40:** Reject for now (log with reason).

## Rules
- An eligibility hard-fail (❌ on any mandatory requirement) caps the composite at 39 regardless of other scores — route to Reject or Relationship-build.
- Unverified status/deadline caps the composite at 59 until verified.
- Ties break on: (1) speed to apply, (2) strategic value, (3) probability.
