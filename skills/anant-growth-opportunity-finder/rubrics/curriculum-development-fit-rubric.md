# Curriculum Development Fit Rubric

Evaluates whether a funder is likely to support curriculum development. Score each 0–5 (0 = no evidence; 3 = adjacent; 5 = explicit and recently funded). Total: 0–50.

## Interpretation
- **40–50: Direct curriculum funder** — pitch content creation itself as the deliverable.
- **30–39: Strong education fit** — pitch curriculum inside a program wrapper (cohorts, workshops, outcomes).
- **20–29: Possible training fit** — pitch delivery and beneficiary outcomes; curriculum is the enabling cost.
- **10–19: Weak fit** — curriculum likely an ineligible cost; verify before writing.
- **0–9: Not relevant.**

## Common Pitfall
Many education funders pay for *delivery* (people served) but not *development* (content creation). If rows 1 and 8 score low but 2–5 score high, budget curriculum work as program staffing within a delivery grant — and confirm allowability with the funder.
