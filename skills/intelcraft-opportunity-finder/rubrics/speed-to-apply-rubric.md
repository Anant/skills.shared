# Speed-to-Apply Rubric (Levels 1–5)

Classify every opportunity by realistic total effort for a prepared applicant. Use the higher level when in doubt.

## Level 1: Same Day
- Simple form
- No budget or attachments
- Short narrative
- Rolling or immediate submission

## Level 2: 1–3 Days
- Short proposal
- Basic budget
- Organization information
- Minimal attachments

## Level 3: 1 Week
- Full narrative
- Budget
- Work plan
- Some supporting documents

## Level 4: 2–4 Weeks
- Complex application
- Partner letters
- Detailed budget
- Evaluation plan
- Registration requirements

## Level 5: 1–3 Months
- Major federal or institutional grant
- Complex compliance
- Multiple partners
- Extensive documentation

## Conversion to Speed Score (for the fit-scoring composite)
| Level | Speed points (0–10) |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 10 |
| 2 | 8 |
| 3 | 6 |
| 4 | 4 |
| 5 | 2 |

## Adjustments
- +1 level if registrations (e.g., federal entity registration) are not yet in place.
- +1 level if letters of support must be requested from partners not yet engaged.
- −1 level (min 1) if a near-identical proposal already exists and can be adapted.
