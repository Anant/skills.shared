# General Grant and Fellowship Databases

> **Verification reminder:** Aggregators lag reality. Treat every listing as a lead, not a fact. Confirm status, deadline, and eligibility on the funder's primary page before reporting.

Each entry: what it is, what it's useful for, typical opportunity types, best-fit themes, application-complexity notes, and a link placeholder to verify.

## Candid (Foundation Directory)
- **Type:** Foundation research database (paid; some free access via libraries/Funding Information Network sites)
- **Useful for:** Identifying foundations by subject, geography, and grant size; seeing historical grants
- **Typical opportunities:** Foundation grants of all sizes
- **Best-fit themes:** Any nonprofit theme; strong for education, community, equity
- **Complexity notes:** Research tool, not an application portal; complexity depends on funder
- **Link:** [verify current URL — candid.org]

## Instrumentl
- **Type:** Grant discovery and matching platform (paid, free trial)
- **Useful for:** Active, deadline-driven grant matching for nonprofits
- **Typical opportunities:** Foundation and government grants
- **Best-fit themes:** Education, workforce, technology, community
- **Complexity notes:** Good deadline data but still verify at source
- **Link:** [verify — instrumentl.com]

## GrantStation
- **Type:** Grant database (paid; often discounted through associations)
- **Useful for:** US and international grantmaker profiles, weekly funding alerts
- **Typical opportunities:** Foundation, corporate, and federal grants
- **Best-fit themes:** Broad nonprofit
- **Complexity notes:** Profiles summarize; always confirm on funder site
- **Link:** [verify — grantstation.com]

## GrantForward
- **Type:** Research funding database (paid; common at universities)
- **Useful for:** Academic and research funding, sponsor discovery
- **Typical opportunities:** Research grants, fellowships
- **Best-fit themes:** Research, university partnerships, AI research
- **Complexity notes:** Academic grants often complex (institutional sign-off)
- **Link:** [verify — grantforward.com]

## Philanthropy News Digest (PND) RFPs
- **Type:** Free RFP listing service from Candid
- **Useful for:** Currently announced RFPs across sectors
- **Typical opportunities:** Foundation RFPs, prizes
- **Best-fit themes:** Broad; education and technology RFPs appear regularly
- **Complexity notes:** Listings link to primary sources — follow them
- **Link:** [verify — philanthropynewsdigest.org/rfps]

## Inside Philanthropy
- **Type:** Funder journalism + funder profiles (paid)
- **Useful for:** Understanding funder strategy, staff, and giving patterns; "who funds X" articles
- **Typical opportunities:** Intelligence, not listings
- **Best-fit themes:** AI and society, education, democracy — has dedicated coverage
- **Complexity notes:** Use to craft positioning and relationship strategy
- **Link:** [verify — insidephilanthropy.com]

## Prize and Challenge Platforms
- **Challenge.gov** — US federal prize competitions; free; eligibility often broad (individuals allowed). [verify — challenge.gov]
- **XPRIZE** — large-scale global competitions; long timelines, high effort. [verify — xprize.org]
- **MIT Solve** — social-impact challenge platform with funding pools; moderate effort; strong for education/AI themes. [verify — solve.mit.edu]
- **HeroX** — crowdsourced challenges of varying size; effort varies. [verify — herox.com]
- **Devpost** — hackathons and developer challenges; fast to enter; small-to-mid prizes; good for open-source/AI tooling. [verify — devpost.com]
- **Kaggle** — data science/ML competitions; relevant when the project has a modeling component. [verify — kaggle.com]
- **Civic innovation challenge portals** — city/state innovation offices and university-run civic challenges; search "[city/state] innovation challenge." [verify per program]
