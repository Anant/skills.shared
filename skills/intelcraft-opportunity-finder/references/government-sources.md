# Government Funding Sources (US Federal, State, Local)

> **Verification reminder:** Federal programs shift with budgets and administrations. Always verify the current notice of funding opportunity (NOFO) on the agency page or Grants.gov, including registration requirements and deadlines. Registrations (e.g., SAM.gov, agency portals) can take weeks — factor into speed-to-apply.

## Federal Portals

### Grants.gov
- **Type:** Central federal grant database and application portal (free)
- **Useful for:** All federal discretionary grants; email alerts by keyword/CFDA
- **Typical opportunities:** Grants and cooperative agreements, small to very large
- **Best-fit themes:** Education, workforce, research, digital equity, innovation
- **Complexity notes:** Level 4–5 speed-to-apply; requires SAM.gov registration for organizations
- **Link:** [verify — grants.gov]

### SAM.gov
- **Type:** Federal registration + contract opportunities (free)
- **Useful for:** Entity registration (prerequisite for federal awards); contract and cooperative agreement notices
- **Complexity notes:** Registration alone can take 2–6 weeks; renew annually
- **Link:** [verify — sam.gov]

## Federal Agencies (highest relevance to AI education / governance / research)

### NSF (National Science Foundation)
- **Useful for:** AI research, education research, workforce (e.g., education/human-resource directorates), SBIR/STTR for small businesses, research infrastructure
- **Best-fit themes:** AI research, STEM education, responsible computing, research training
- **Complexity notes:** Level 5; institutional eligibility common; SBIR open to for-profits
- **Link:** [verify — nsf.gov/funding]

### NIH (National Institutes of Health)
- **Useful for:** AI in health, data science training, workforce diversity programs
- **Complexity notes:** Level 5; mostly institutional
- **Link:** [verify — grants.nih.gov]

### Department of Education
- **Useful for:** Adult education, education innovation and research, postsecondary programs, edtech
- **Best-fit themes:** Curriculum, adult learning, digital literacy
- **Complexity notes:** Level 4–5
- **Link:** [verify — ed.gov + grants.gov]

### Department of Labor
- **Useful for:** Workforce development, apprenticeships, reskilling/upskilling grants
- **Best-fit themes:** AI workforce readiness, training providers
- **Complexity notes:** Level 4–5; often requires workforce-system partners
- **Link:** [verify — dol.gov/grants]

### Department of Commerce / EDA
- **Useful for:** Economic development, tech hubs, good-jobs and innovation programs
- **Complexity notes:** Level 5; regional coalitions favored
- **Link:** [verify — commerce.gov, eda.gov]

### NIST
- **Useful for:** AI standards, measurement, trustworthy-AI programs, manufacturing extension
- **Best-fit themes:** AI governance, evaluation, standards education
- **Link:** [verify — nist.gov]

### NTIA
- **Useful for:** Digital equity, broadband adoption, digital inclusion programs (often flowed through states)
- **Best-fit themes:** Digital literacy, community AI capacity
- **Link:** [verify — ntia.gov and state digital equity offices]

### IMLS (Institute of Museum and Library Services)
- **Useful for:** Library/museum-partnered digital literacy and community learning
- **Best-fit themes:** Community AI literacy via libraries
- **Complexity notes:** Level 3–4; partnership with a library/museum often required
- **Link:** [verify — imls.gov]

### NEH / NEA
- **Useful for:** Humanities + technology (NEH digital humanities), arts + tech (NEA)
- **Best-fit themes:** AI and society, ethics education, public humanities
- **Complexity notes:** Level 3–4
- **Link:** [verify — neh.gov, arts.gov]

### SBA
- **Useful for:** Small-business support programs, growth accelerator prize competitions
- **Complexity notes:** Prize competitions can be Level 2–3
- **Link:** [verify — sba.gov]

### National labs and research agencies (DOE, DARPA, IARPA, etc.)
- **Useful for:** Advanced AI research, evaluation, security-relevant work
- **Complexity notes:** Level 5; specialized eligibility
- **Link:** [verify per agency]
